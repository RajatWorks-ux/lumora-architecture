Our Studio

Where architecture meets emotion

Founded in 2012, LUMORA is an international architecture and interior design studio with ateliers in Paris, London, and New York. We believe that exceptional spaces are not merely built — they are felt.

Our approach is rooted in a deep understanding of how light moves through space, how materials age with grace, and how proportion creates harmony. Each project begins with listening — to the site, to the client, to the context — and evolves through rigorous iteration into environments that feel inevitable.

We work across residential, hospitality, and cultural typologies, collaborating with artisans, engineers, and craftspeople who share our obsession with detail. The result is architecture that transcends trend, offering enduring value and emotional resonance.

Design Philosophy

Principles that guide every decision

01

Light as Material

We design for the movement of light — its quality, direction, and transformation throughout the day and seasons. Light reveals texture, defines volume, and creates atmosphere.

02

Material Honesty

Materials are expressed truthfully — stone as stone, wood as wood, concrete as concrete. We celebrate patina, grain, and the beauty of aging.

03

Inside-Outside Continuity

The boundary between interior and landscape is deliberately blurred. Gardens, courtyards, and terraces extend the living space into nature.

04

Timeless Proportion

We draw on classical principles of harmony and ratio, adapted for contemporary life. Spaces feel right because their proportions are right.

05

Craft as Expression

Every detail is an opportunity for artistry. We collaborate with master craftspeople whose hands bring soul to our drawings.

06

Silence & Sanctuary

In a noisy world, we design for acoustic comfort and visual calm — spaces that offer refuge and restoration.
