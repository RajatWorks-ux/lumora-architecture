1. Introduction

This Cookie Policy explains how LUMORA ("we," "our," "us") uses cookies and similar tracking technologies on our website lumora.example.com. It explains what these technologies are, why we use them, and your rights to control their use.

This policy should be read alongside our Privacy Policy, which provides additional context about how we process personal data.

2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile) when you visit a website. They allow the website to recognize your device and remember information about your visit, such as preferences, login status, and browsing activity.

Similar technologies include web beacons, pixels, local storage, and session storage. For simplicity, this policy uses "cookies" to refer to all such technologies.

3. Types of Cookies We Use

We use the following categories of cookies:

3.1 Essential Cookies (Strictly Necessary)

These cookies are required for the website to function properly and cannot be disabled. They enable core functionality such as:

  • Session management and security
  • Cookie consent preference storage
  • Form submission and CSRF protection
  • Load balancing and infrastructure

Legal basis: Legitimate interest (website functionality)

3.2 Analytics Cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website by collecting anonymous usage data:

  • Pages visited and time spent
  • Navigation paths and exit pages
  • Geographic location (country/city level)
  • Device and browser information
  • Traffic sources (search, direct, referral)

We use Google Analytics (GA4) for this purpose. Data is anonymized via IP masking.

Legal basis: Consent

3.3 Functional Cookies

These cookies enable enhanced functionality and personalization:

  • Language and region preferences
  • Font size and display preferences
  • Video playback settings
  • Form auto-fill data

Legal basis: Consent

3.4 Marketing Cookies

We do not currently use marketing or advertising cookies on our website.

5. Third-Party Cookies

We use Google Analytics (GA4) which sets cookies on our domain. Google's privacy policy governs their use of data collected via these cookies. We have enabled IP anonymization and disabled data sharing for advertising purposes.

No other third-party cookies (e.g., advertising networks, social media pixels) are currently used on our website.

6. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

6.1 Cookie Consent Banner

When you first visit our website, a cookie consent banner appears allowing you to:

  • Accept All: Consent to all cookie categories
  • Reject Optional: Allow only essential cookies

You can change your preferences at any time by clicking the "Cookie Preferences" link in the footer or clearing your browser cookies to re-trigger the banner.

6.2 Browser Controls

Most browsers allow you to:

  • View and delete stored cookies
  • Block all cookies or third-party cookies
  • Receive alerts when cookies are set
  • Automatically clear cookies on browser close

Note: Disabling essential cookies may cause the website to function improperly.

7. Cookie Retention

Cookies are retained for the durations specified in the table above. You can manually delete cookies at any time through your browser settings.
