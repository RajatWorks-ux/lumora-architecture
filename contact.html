Frequently Asked

Common questions

How do you select projects?

We take on a limited number of projects each year — typically 8-12 — to ensure each receives the depth of attention it deserves. Selection is based on alignment with our design philosophy, project complexity, client engagement, and schedule compatibility. We're transparent about capacity and will refer trusted colleagues when we're at capacity.

What is your fee structure?

Fees are project-specific and typically structured as a percentage of construction cost (8-15% depending on scope) or a fixed fee for defined phases. We provide a detailed fee proposal after the initial discovery phase. We do not work on speculative or unpaid competition basis.

Do you work internationally?

Yes. Our ateliers in Paris, London, and New York support projects globally. We have delivered work across Europe, the Americas, the Middle East, and Asia. For projects outside our office locations, we establish local partnerships for regulatory compliance and site presence.

What is the typical project timeline?

Residential projects: 18-36 months from concept to completion. Hospitality: 24-48 months. Cultural: 36-60+ months. Consultation engagements: 4-12 weeks. Timelines vary significantly based on scale, complexity, jurisdiction, and client decision-making pace.

Can we visit your studio?

Absolutely. We welcome clients to our Paris atelier by appointment. It's the best way to experience our material library, see current models, and meet the team. Virtual studio tours are also available for international clients.

Do you offer interior design only?

Yes. We accept interior-only commissions for existing structures where the architectural shell is fixed. These projects follow the same rigorous process — concept through FF&E procurement and installation.
