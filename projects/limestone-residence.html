The Limestone Residence

A coastal villa where warm limestone meets expansive glazing, blurring the boundary between interior and Mediterranean horizon.

Typology Private Residence
Location Côte d'Azur, France
Status Conceptual Design
Year 2024

Project Overview

Perched on a gentle promontory overlooking the Mediterranean, The Limestone Residence is conceived as a dialogue between geology and horizon. The design draws its material vocabulary from the site itself — warm, fossil-rich limestone quarried from the surrounding hills.

The residence unfolds as a sequence of indoor and outdoor rooms, each framed by floor-to-ceiling glazing that disappears into the structure. The boundary between inside and outside dissolves: living spaces extend onto deep terraces sheltered by cantilevered stone soffits, while an infinity pool appears to merge with the sea beyond.

Internally, a restrained palette of honed limestone, white oak, and natural linen creates a backdrop for living. Custom joinery, sculptural lighting, and carefully selected artworks complete the composition. The master suite occupies the entire upper level, with a private terrace oriented toward the sunrise.

Design Concept

Stone as protagonist

The concept emerges from a simple observation: the site is limestone. Rather than importing foreign materials, the architecture grows from the ground it occupies.

The facade is composed of hand-dressed limestone blocks, each unique in its fossil inclusions and tonal variation. The stone is laid in a randomized ashlar pattern that echoes the stratigraphy of the quarry itself. Deep reveals around windows create shadow lines that animate the facade throughout the day.

Structurally, the residence employs a reinforced concrete frame with limestone cladding — a contemporary interpretation of the region's traditional stone construction. Cantilevered terraces extend up to 4 meters, their undersides clad in the same limestone, creating a sense of geological continuity.

The pool is not merely adjacent to the architecture — it is carved from it. The pool's edge aligns precisely with the living room threshold, its infinity edge calibrated to visually merge with the Mediterranean horizon 200 meters below.
