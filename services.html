Our Expertise

Comprehensive design services

From concept to completion, we guide every aspect of the spatial experience. Each service is delivered with the same rigor, craft, and attention to detail that defines our work.

Service

Architecture

New builds, extensions, and masterplanning — from private villas to cultural institutions.

Our architectural practice spans the full project lifecycle — from site analysis and feasibility through concept design, planning, technical resolution, and construction administration. We work across typologies: private residences, boutique hotels, cultural buildings, and commercial projects.

Every project begins with deep site immersion — understanding topography, climate, light, history, and regulatory context. We develop concepts through iterative physical modeling and hand drawing before moving to digital documentation. Our technical team ensures that design intent survives the transition to built reality.

Scope Includes

  • Feasibility studies & site appraisal
  • Concept & schematic design
  • Planning & regulatory approvals
  • Design development & technical design
  • Construction documentation
  • Tender & contractor selection
  • Construction administration
  • Post-occupancy evaluation

Typologies

  • Private residences & estates
  • Boutique hotels & resorts
  • Cultural & civic buildings
  • Commercial & workplace
  • Masterplanning & urban design

Service

Interior Design

Holistic interior environments — furniture, lighting, textiles, and bespoke joinery.

We design interiors as complete spatial experiences, not merely decorated surfaces. Every element — from the macro layout to the micro detail of a door handle — is considered in relation to the whole. Our in-house workshop prototypes custom furniture, lighting, and joinery; our textile studio develops exclusive fabrics.

We collaborate with master craftspeople worldwide: cabinetmakers in Italy, weavers in Morocco, ceramicists in Japan, metalsmiths in the UK. The result is interiors with depth, authenticity, and a sense of collected history — even in brand-new spaces.

Scope Includes

  • Space planning & layout
  • FF&E specification & procurement
  • Bespoke furniture & joinery design
  • Lighting design & specification
  • Textile & soft furnishings design
  • Art curation & placement
  • Color & material palettes
  • Styling & final installation

Specializations

  • Residential interiors
  • Hotel & hospitality interiors
  • Retail & showroom design
  • Yacht & aviation interiors
  • Historic interior restoration

Service

Residential Design

Tailored homes that reflect the rhythms and rituals of daily life.

A home is the most personal architecture there is. Our residential design service goes beyond aesthetics to understand how our clients actually live — their routines, gatherings, solitude, and aspirations. We design for the morning light on the breakfast table, the acoustics of a family dinner, the privacy of a home office, the joy of an outdoor shower.

We offer a full continuum from new-build villas to apartment renovations, from primary residences to holiday homes. Each project receives a dedicated design team and a bespoke process calibrated to the client's engagement level — from highly collaborative to fully delegated.

Scope Includes

  • Brief development & lifestyle analysis
  • Architectural design (new build or renovation)
  • Interior architecture & design
  • Landscape & outdoor space design
  • Wellness spaces (spa, gym, pool)
  • Smart home & technology integration
  • Art & collection integration
  • Staff & service quarters design

Project Types

  • Primary residences
  • Secondary & holiday homes
  • Urban apartments & penthouses
  • Country estates & villas
  • Multi-generational compounds

Service

Hospitality Design

Boutique hotels, resorts, and restaurants that create memorable guest experiences.

Hospitality design is choreography — the guest's journey from arrival to departure, from public to private, from day to night. We design for emotional resonance: the first glimpse of the lobby, the scent in the corridor, the texture of the bed linen, the view from the bath. Every touchpoint is an opportunity to deepen the guest's connection to place.

We work with developers, operators, and brands from concept through pre-opening. Our team includes specialists in hotel planning, F&B design, spa & wellness, and brand experience. We understand the commercial realities — RevPAR, ADR, operational efficiency — and design for both guest delight and owner return.

Scope Includes

  • Concept development & brand alignment
  • Masterplanning & guest journey mapping
  • Architectural & interior design
  • F&B venue design (restaurants, bars, room service)
  • Spa, wellness & fitness design
  • Public realm & landscape design
  • FF&E & OS&E procurement
  • Pre-opening & operational support

Typologies

  • Boutique hotels (10-100 keys)
  • Luxury resorts & villas
  • Wellness retreats
  • Restaurants & bars
  • Private members' clubs

Service

Renovation & Restoration

Sensitive transformation of heritage properties and existing structures.

We approach existing buildings with humility and rigor. Whether a listed monument, a mid-century villa, or a forgotten warehouse, we seek to understand the building's logic — its structure, its history, its scars — before proposing change. Our interventions are legible: new is distinguishable from old, yet harmonious.

We have deep expertise in traditional materials and techniques: lime plaster, stone masonry, timber framing, decorative plasterwork, historic joinery. We collaborate with conservation officers, heritage bodies, and specialist craftspeople to ensure compliance and authenticity.

Scope Includes

  • Condition surveys & heritage assessments
  • Conservation & repair strategies
  • Adaptive reuse & reprogramming
  • Structural strengthening & stabilization
  • Traditional craft repair (plaster, stone, timber, metal)
  • Modern MEP integration in historic fabric
  • Listed building consent & planning
  • Phased delivery for occupied buildings

Expertise Areas

  • Historic residential restoration
  • Heritage hotel conversion
  • Cultural building adaptation
  • Facade retention & restoration
  • Interior decorative restoration

Spatial Consultation

Strategic advisory for property developers, investors, and institutions.

Not every engagement requires full design services. Our consultation offering provides expert spatial intelligence at critical decision points — acquisition, planning, positioning, or optimization. We bring architectural thinking to strategic problems.

Our consultants combine design expertise with commercial acumen. We've advised on billion-euro development pipelines, heritage portfolio strategies, brand rollout feasibility, and single-site acquisitions. The output is actionable: reports, diagrams, financial models, and clear recommendations.

Scope Includes

  • Site acquisition due diligence
  • Development feasibility & massing studies
  • Planning strategy & risk assessment
  • Brand & concept positioning
  • Design quality audits
  • Value engineering & cost optimization
  • Portfolio strategy & asset optimization
  • Expert witness & dispute resolution

Client Types

  • Property developers & investors
  • Hotel operators & brands
  • Cultural institutions
  • Family offices & HNWIs
  • Government & public bodies
