Our Studio

Where architecture meets emotion

Founded in 2012, LUMORA is an international architecture and interior design studio with ateliers in Paris, London, and New York. We believe exceptional spaces are not merely built — they are felt.

Design Philosophy

Six principles that guide every decision

01

Light as Material

We design for the movement of light — its quality, direction, and transformation throughout the day and seasons. Light reveals texture, defines volume, and creates atmosphere. A space that breathes with the sun is a space that feels alive.

02

Material Honesty

Materials are expressed truthfully — stone as stone, wood as wood, concrete as concrete. We celebrate patina, grain, and the beauty of aging. Nothing is disguised; everything is revealed. Authenticity creates trust between space and occupant.

03

Inside-Outside Continuity

The boundary between interior and landscape is deliberately blurred. Gardens, courtyards, and terraces extend the living space into nature. Transitions are choreographed — threshold moments that heighten awareness of the environment.

04

Timeless Proportion

We draw on classical principles of harmony and ratio — the golden section, the Fibonacci sequence, modular systems — adapted for contemporary life. Spaces feel right because their proportions are right. Harmony is felt before it is understood.

05

Craft as Expression

Every detail is an opportunity for artistry. We collaborate with master craftspeople — stone carvers, cabinetmakers, metalsmiths, textile artists — whose hands bring soul to our drawings. The maker's mark is not hidden; it is celebrated.

06

Silence & Sanctuary

In a noisy world, we design for acoustic comfort and visual calm — spaces that offer refuge and restoration. Thick walls, deep reveals, layered textiles, and strategic planting create sanctuaries where the mind can settle.

Our Approach

How we work

Every project begins with listening — to the site, to the client, to the context. We do not impose a signature style; we uncover the project's essential nature.

01

Discovery & Immersion

We spend time on site, understanding light, climate, topography, history, and culture. We listen deeply to our clients' aspirations, rituals, and dreams.

02

Concept Development

Through hand sketching, physical models, and material studies, we explore multiple conceptual directions. Ideas are tested, refined, and distilled to their essence.

03

Design Resolution

Technical rigor meets artistic vision. We coordinate structure, systems, and detailing with consultants while maintaining design integrity at every scale.

04

Construction & Curation

We remain deeply involved through construction — site visits, mock-ups, material approvals. Final curation of furnishings, art, and objects completes the spatial narrative.

Our Values

What we stand for

Integrity

We honor our commitments — to clients, to craftspeople, to the environment. Our word is our bond.

Longevity

We design for generations, not seasons. Durability, adaptability, and timeless beauty are non-negotiable.

Collaboration

The best work emerges from dialogue. We listen to clients, consultants, craftspeople, and the land itself.

Stewardship

We are temporary custodians of the built environment. Every decision considers its impact on future generations.
